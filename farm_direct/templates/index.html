The Real Problem & Our Direct Solution

Traditional agricultural supply chains drain wealth from hard-working farmers and inflate consumer costs.

❌ The Traditional Middleman Model

  • ❌ Multiple Mediators: Agents, wholesalers, sub-wholesalers, and retailers all take hefty cuts.
  • ❌ Farmer Underpaid: Farmers often receive as little as 30% of what the consumer pays.
  • ❌ Consumer Overcharged: Final retail price inflated by 50% to 150% due to middleman markups.
  • ❌ Lack of Freshness: Produce sits in intermediate godowns and cold chains for days.
  • ❌ Zero Transparency: Neither the farmer knows who bought, nor consumer knows who grew it.

✅ The FarmDirect Direct Model

  • ✅ Zero Middlemen: 100% direct connection between grower and consumer.
  • ✅ 100% Farmer Earnings: Farmer sets the honest price and keeps every single rupee.
  • ✅ Big Savings for Consumers: Pay honest farm rates without broker commissions.
  • ✅ Harvest-Fresh Quality: Picked fresh and delivered without intermediate storage delays.
  • ✅ Direct WhatsApp & Call: Direct phone/chat connection for instant coordination.
